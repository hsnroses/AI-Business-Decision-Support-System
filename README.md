# AI Business Decision Support System

## Project Description

This project aims to develop an AI-powered business decision support system that analyses petrol station sales data, identifies sales trends, forecasts future sales, and provides useful business insights through a dashboard and AI assistant.

## Problem Statement

Businesses collect sales data from different sources, but analysing the data to understand business performance and predict future sales can be challenging. Business owners may find it difficult to identify sales trends, compare the performance of different income sources, and use historical data to support business decisions. This project explores the use of machine learning and AI to analyse business data, forecast future sales, and explain the results in simple language.

## Evidence of the Problem

- The UCI Online Retail dataset provides transaction data that can be used to investigate sales trends and customer purchasing patterns.
- The Sample Superstore dataset provides sales, profit, discount, and product information for business performance analysis.
- Time-series forecasting research explores methods for predicting future values using historical data.

## Initial Related Work

1. Microsoft Power BI — a business intelligence tool for analysing data and presenting interactive reports and dashboards.
2. Tableau — a data visualisation platform for exploring business data and identifying trends.
3. Machine learning-based sales forecasting — methods that use historical sales data to predict future sales.

The capabilities and limitations of these approaches will be investigated further during the literature review.

## Engineering Gap

This project aims to integrate business data processing, machine learning-based sales forecasting, and AI-generated explanations into a single application. The system will allow users to explore sales performance, view future sales predictions, and understand the results through an interactive dashboard and conversational interface.

## Initial Project Objectives

1. Develop a data processing pipeline to clean and analyse business sales data.
2. Develop and evaluate a machine learning model for forecasting future sales.
3. Integrate sales analysis, forecasting results, and AI-generated explanations into a dashboard to support business decision-making.

## Target Users

- Small and medium-sized business owners
- Petrol station operators and management
- Business analysts
- Students and researchers in AI and business analytics

## Proposed Data Sources

- Actual petrol station sales data, subject to permission and data availability.
- UCI Online Retail dataset: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online+retail
- Sample Superstore dataset: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets?search=sample+superstore

The actual petrol station dataset will be the preferred data source if sufficient historical sales records are available. Public datasets may be used for initial development and testing if needed.

## Responsible AI Risks

- Inaccurate sales forecasts may lead to poor business decisions.
- Incomplete or inconsistent sales records may affect model performance.
- AI-generated explanations may misinterpret results if they are not grounded in calculated data.
- Confidential business information must be protected and used only with appropriate permission.
- Forecasts should be presented as estimates rather than guaranteed future sales.
